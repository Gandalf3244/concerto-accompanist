# Concerto Accompanist

Plays the orchestra of Prokofiev's Piano Concerto No. 3 (first movement) while
you play the piano part, following your tempo. Open the site in Safari on an
iPhone or iPad and use Share › Add to Home Screen; it works offline after the
first visit.

The score was read from the public-domain Boosey & Hawkes full score by our own
optical music recognition; instrument sounds are rendered from the MuseScore
General SoundFont (MIT licence).
